# Second Hohenberg-Kohn Theorem
> 能量泛函在基態密度有最小值：其他密度都給出更高的能量〔second Hohenberg-Kohn theorem〕

curve: $\rho$ || $E_{V_{ext}}[\rho]$ || $\rho_0$〔second Hohenberg-Kohn theorem〕

$$
E_{V_{ext}}[\rho]=F_{HK}[\rho]+\int\rho(\vec r)\,V_{ext}(\vec r)\,d\vec r
