# DFT Basics: From Many Electrons to One Density
> DFT 把難解的 many-body Schrödinger equation 轉換成一組 single-particle Kohn-Sham equations，並以 electron density 作為核心變數〔density functional theory〕

$$
\hat H\,\Psi(\vec r_1,\dots,\vec r_N)=E\,\Psi(\vec r_1,\dots,\vec r_N)
\;\xrightarrow{\ \text{DFT}\ }\;
\hat H_{KS}\,\phi(\vec r)=E\,\phi(\vec r)
$$
