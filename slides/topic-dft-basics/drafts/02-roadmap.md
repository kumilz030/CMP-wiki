# Roadmap: Three Levels of Approximation
> 從精確的 Hamiltonian 出發，經過三層近似，最後才得到預測〔three levels of approximation〕

flow: exact hamiltonian -> level 1: Born-Oppenheimer -> level 2: Hartree-Fock 或 DFT -> level 3: solution techniques -> prediction〔three levels of approximation〕

- 這張圖在課程多個主題的開頭重複出現，用來標示該主題處於哪一層〔three levels of approximation〕
- level 2 有兩條路：Hartree-Fock（及 post-Hartree-Fock）與 density functional theory〔three levels of approximation〕
- level 3 是數值求解方法，例如 basis set 與 self-consistent field〔three levels of approximation〕
- 這份簡報沿著 DFT 這條路，從 level 1 走到 level 3〔three levels of approximation〕
